🧪 Try This! — The Glitter Germs Test

Germs are too tiny to see. So we will use glitter as our pretend germs, and watch how far they can travel!

  1. Your teacher puts a little glitter on one child's hands. This glitter is our pretend germs.
  2. That child shakes hands with a friend. Look carefully at the friend's hands.
  3. Now that friend touches a book and a cup. Look for glitter on them too.
  4. Wash your hands well with soap and water. Count slowly to twenty as you wash.
  5. Look at your hands again. The glitter germs are gone!
